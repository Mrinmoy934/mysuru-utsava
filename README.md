## 🌿 About Mysuru Utsava

Mysuru Utsava is a celebration of conscious living, sustainability, traditional craftsmanship, wellness & mindful consumption. Bringing together eco-friendly products, handcrafted creations, traditional artisans, natural products, and conscious brands from across India.

### 🚫 Strict Sustainability Policy
- **Only conscious & sustainable brands.**
- **No powerloom, fast fashion, or plastic-based products.**

## 🛍️ Featured Categories

- Handloom & Traditional Textiles

- Natural Skincare & Wellness

- Upcycled & Recycled Products
