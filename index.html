What is it?

More than a market.

Mysuru Utsava brings together tradition, craft, food and performance under one roof, curated with intention rather than mass-produced excess.

Across three days, wander through handmade goods, taste local food, watch live performances, and take home a little more culture than you came with. Part bazaar, part festival, made for people who like to shop with meaning and celebrate while they're at it.

Tradition Celebration Event Fun & Entertainment

Six worlds, one venue.

Every stall is chosen with intention: real makers, honest materials, and stories worth knowing.

01 CRAFT

Art & Handicrafts

Pottery and terracotta, bamboo & cane work, hand-painted decor, macrame, block printing and handloom textile art, made by the artisans themselves.

02 WEAR

Apparel

Sarees and ethnic wear, indie fashion labels, upcycled and refashioned clothing, occasion wear, traditional roots with a contemporary edge.

03 ADORN

Jewellery

Handcrafted silver and oxidised pieces, terracotta and fabric jewellery, and contemporary artisan designs for everyday and festive wear.

04 MINDFUL

Conscious & Sustainable

Upcycled and zero-waste goods, natural dyes, organic skincare, reusable everyday products, made to last, not to be thrown away.

05 TASTE

Eatables

Local flavours, homemade food, health-conscious snacks and traditional festival treats from small kitchens.

06 LIVE

Experiences & Performances

Live music and dance, cultural acts on stage, and hands-on workshops that let you make something with your own hands.

For Vendors & Entrepreneurs

Built to support local makers.

Women entrepreneurs, men entrepreneurs, passion-led makers who turned a hobby into a business, and young or first-time entrepreneurs just getting started. Get real support, reach the right audience, and build your brand with genuine in-person exposure.

For Visitors

Shop with meaning.

Meet the makers behind what you buy, discover local and sustainable brands you won't find in a mall, and spend a day surrounded by food, music and craft. Come to shop, come to eat, come to watch a performance, or just come to see what Mysuru's small businesses are creating.
