Celebrating People, Planet & Culture

Experience. Connect. Be The Change.

Green Guru Youth Foundation proudly presents Mysuru Utsava 2026 — a conscious bazaar reimagined for people who value authenticity, heritage, and environmental harmony.

Over two vibrant days at JCAC Mysuru, discover handloom textiles, handmade jewellery, natural skincare, sustainable home decor, and conscious food from passionate artisans across India.

Traditional Craftsmanship Conscious Living Eco-friendly Products Arts & Culture

What We Are Looking For

Curated for conscious brands, eco-friendly creators, and traditional Indian artisans from across India.

01 TEXTILES

Handloom & Textiles

Traditional weaves, hand-spun textiles, organic cottons, and authentic Indian handloom apparel. (No powerloom or fast fashion).

02 JEWELLERY

Handmade Jewellery

Handcrafted silver, oxidized metal, terracotta, thread, and upcycled artisan jewellery designs.

03 DÉCOR

Sustainable Home Décor

Bamboo & cane decor, hand-painted art, terracotta crafts, clayware, and eco-friendly home embellishments.

04 WELLNESS

Natural Skincare & Wellness

Organic soaps, natural remedies, botanical skincare, essential oils, and plastic-free wellness products.

05 LIFESTYLE

Eco Lifestyle Products

Zero-waste daily utilities, jute & cloth bags, compostable goods, and earth-friendly lifestyle alternatives.

06 UPCYCLED

Upcycled & Recycled

Creative products handcrafted from repurposed fabric, reclaimed wood, and recycled waste materials.

07 ARTISANS

Traditional Artisans

Heritage folk art, traditional wood carvings, pottery, and indigenous Indian craft forms.

08 FOOD

Conscious Food

Organic snacks, cold-pressed treats, traditional culinary delights, and natural farm produce.
